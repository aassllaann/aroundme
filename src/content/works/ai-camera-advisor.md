## Designing for a decision, not a result

Camera Advisor is a UI/UX redesign created in **Figma** and derived from the functional **Agent Product Comparison** prototype. This focused mobile flow asks for a budget, intended use, and priorities, then presents three options with the reasoning needed to compare them.

## Interaction structure

- **Need:** begin with the user's own language instead of a long specification form.
- **Interpretation:** expose the budget and priorities detected by the system before showing an answer.
- **Recommendation:** frame one option through its best use and main trade-off rather than presenting a score alone.
- **Alternatives:** keep the other candidates visible in the same comparison structure.
- **Reasoning:** separate portability, video, and low-light performance so the recommendation can be questioned.

## Visual direction

The interface uses sharp geometric fields, black space, saturated pink, pale paper panels, and a dark green counterpoint. Expressive display type gives the experience a playful editorial voice, while monospaced text keeps specifications and system reasoning readable.

## Scope and evidence

This entry documents the supplied five-screen UI/UX prototype. Product names, specifications, scores, and prices are scenario content shown in the prototype, not current purchasing advice. No user-testing outcome is claimed here.
