## Botanical character concepts

<div class="character-design-gallery character-design-gallery--concepts">
  <figure><img src="/images/works/character-design-studies/aeginetia-indica.png" alt="The Burrower, a botanical character concept based on Aeginetia indica" loading="lazy" /><figcaption><strong>The Burrower</strong><p>A shy and thin monster that lives under the ground. It does not need food or air. When a player walks too close, it comes out of the soil, grabs them, and uses them as a host to take their health.</p><em>Watch your step.</em></figcaption></figure>
  <figure><img src="/images/works/character-design-studies/amorphophallus-paeoniifolius.png" alt="The Smelly Corpse, a botanical character concept based on Amorphophallus paeoniifolius" loading="lazy" /><figcaption><strong>The Smelly Corpse</strong><p>A monster that came back from death. Its body smells like rotten meat, but players smell a sweet flower scent instead. The smell puts players into a dream-like world. If they stay there for too long, their bodies will start to rot.</p><em>Hold your breath.</em></figcaption></figure>
  <figure><img src="/images/works/character-design-studies/nepenthes-rafflesiana.png" alt="The Hungry Chef, a botanical character concept based on Nepenthes rafflesiana" loading="lazy" /><figcaption><strong>The Hungry Chef</strong><p>A monster that looks like a chef and cooks many tasty foods. If a player eats its food, its three tongues will come out and pull the player into its stomach.</p><em>Don’t be greedy.</em></figcaption></figure>
</div>
