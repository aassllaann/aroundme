## A table across time

La Table du Temps is an interactive cultural archive of French cuisine. It connects 5 historical eras, 28 dishes, local images, and relationship types to make culinary history feel navigable rather than encyclopedic.

## What is in the archive

- A narrative entrance that frames the interface as a journey through changing ideas of taste, class, technique, and memory.
- A D3 relationship graph with zoom, pan, node selection, hover states, and relationship explanations.
- An era timeline spanning medieval court cuisine, classical French cuisine, the grand hotel era, Nouvelle Cuisine, and contemporary dining.
- Dish records with French and Chinese names, origins, descriptions, ingredients, attribution, and supported local imagery.
- A second sensory narration layer that reads history through smell, texture, and memory.

## Method

The data model separates eras, dishes, and relationships. A collection workflow uses Wikidata and Wikipedia for structured enrichment and cross-checking, with manual review for historical interpretation and narrative placement.

## Status

The landing page, exploration view, relationship graph, detail system, and collection scripts are implemented. Mobile refinement, final visual captures, deployment, and source-attribution polish remain in progress.
