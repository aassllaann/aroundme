## A first-person record

Photography is not treated here as a reason to travel or an invitation to look. The instant of pressing the shutter is too brief to contain an eternal subject, a complete theory, or an abstract record of time and space.

The images begin from a personal motive: what is noticed before it becomes a picture, and what remains after it has passed.

## Portfolio edition

This 2023—2026 portfolio is presented as a 46-spread digital book. It moves through saturated garden scenes, architectural fragments, water, animals, black-and-white studies, and a closing image of flight.

## Reading mode

Use the page corners, swipe gesture, controls, or arrow keys to move through the original spreads.
